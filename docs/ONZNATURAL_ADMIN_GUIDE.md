## 1. Authentication & Authorization

The ONZNATURAL Admin Dashboard is engineered without mock logins, email-string bypasses, or client-side cookie storage.

### Login Steps:
1. Access `https://onznatural.com/admin`.
2. Enter your authorized staff email and password.
3. The dashboard executes `supabase.auth.signInWithPassword()`.
4. If authentication succeeds, the backend checks the `admin_users` table for an authorized role (`super_admin`, `store_manager`, `support`).
5. If authorized, the session token is loaded and dashboard telemetry is initialized.
6. If unauthorized, the user is signed out immediately and an explicit "Access Denied" notice is presented.

## 2. Module 1: Overview & Performance

The **Overview** tab provides an instant pulse of your studio's operations:
- **Total Customers**: Live count of registered accounts and captured customer profiles.
- **AI Consultations**: Number of completed botanical skincare consultations.
- **Orders & Enquiries**: Total WhatsApp orders and storefront enquiries logged.
- **Pending Follow-Ups**: Highlighted badge indicating orders in `new` status awaiting staff dispatch.
- **Most AI-Recommended Products**: Ranked list of products recommended by Gemini 2.5 Flash during consultations.
- **Most Viewed & Clicked Botanicals**: Ranked list of products based on user views, quick previews, and engagement.

---

## 3. Module 2: Customer Directory

The **Customers** tab manages all customer identities:
- **Distinction of Accounts**: Clearly tags users as **Registered Account** (with full self-service portal access) or **Guest / Lead** (order or enquiry contact).
- **Search & Filters**: Search in real time by customer name, email address, or phone number. Filter by status (`active`, `inactive`, `blocked`).
- **Customer Details Drawer**: View customer registration date, preferred language (`en`, `hi`, `gu`), and unique UUID.
- **Status Toggle**: Update customer status between active and inactive.
- **CSV Export**: Click **Export Customers CSV** to download a spreadsheet with all contact fields.

---

## 4. Module 3: Customer Activity Feed

The **Activity** tab displays an immutable, chronological stream of user interactions captured by your first-party telemetry service:
- **Event Types**:
  - `view_details`: Customer opened a product page.
  - `quick_preview`: Customer opened a quick preview modal from a product card.
  - `wishlist_add`: Customer saved a soap to their botanical wishlist.
  - `order_intent`: Customer clicked "Order on WhatsApp" or "Direct Buy".
- **Session Identification**: Differentiates between authenticated users (`User: <id>`) and anonymous visitors (`Guest: <session>`).
- **Metadata Inspection**: View device type, category context, or referrer without capturing personally invasive cookies.

---

## 5. Module 4: AI Consultation Transcripts

The **AI Chats** tab allows store owners to audit AI conversations:
- **Search & Filter**: Search conversations by customer name, email, or message keyword. Filter by date (`All Dates`, `Today`, `Past 7 Days`).
- **Transcript Viewer**:
  - Left panel shows all consultation sessions with customer names and timestamps.
  - Right panel displays the complete message turn-by-turn dialogue.
  - Shows recommended product tags generated by the AI for each turn.
  - Clearly shows session metadata (`session_id`, `status: active | completed`).
- **CSV Export**: Download full transcripts with customer IDs and summaries for customer care reviews.

## 6. Module 5: Product Interests & Demand Signals

The **Product Interests** tab aggregates high-intent customer actions:
- **Intent Signals**:
  - `order_intent`: Direct intent to purchase via WhatsApp or UPI.
  - `wishlist_add`: Strong intent to save for later purchase.
  - `ai_recommended`: Products matched to user skin concerns by the AI advisor.
- **Actionable Inventory Planning**: Use this data to determine which cold process soap batches need curing next in your Vadodara studio based on customer demand.

---

## 7. Module 6: Central Product Catalogue

The **Catalogue** tab serves as the single source of truth for the entire store:
- **Live Inventory**: All 16 products are listed with photos, categories, net volume, and live pricing.
- **In-Line Editing**:
  - Click **Edit** on any product.
  - Modify the **Price (INR)**.
  - Update the **Availability Status**:
    - `In Stock`: Ready for orders.
    - `Low Stock`: Shows urgency badge on storefront.
    - `Out of Stock`: Disables ordering with friendly notice.
    - `Discontinued`: Hides from active suggestions.
  - Click **Save**.
- **Real-Time Store Sync**: Changes save to Supabase and immediately synchronize with the React product context, updating the Homepage, Collection, and Product Detail pages instantly.

---

## 8. Module 7: Customer Orders & Leads

The **Orders & Leads** tab tracks WhatsApp orders and contact inquiries:
- **Status Workflow**:
  1. `New`: Customer submitted order details or clicked WhatsApp checkout.
  2. `Contacted`: Staff reached out to customer via WhatsApp or phone.
  3. `Resolved`: Payment received and parcel dispatched from Vadodara.
  4. `Closed`: Order completed or archived.
- **One-Click WhatsApp**: Click the green phone icon to open a prefilled chat with the customer directly in WhatsApp Web or mobile.

## 9. Module 8: Right to Erasure (DPDP Privacy)

The **Erasure** tab processes customer data deletion requests in compliance with India's DPDP Act:
- **Pending Requests**: Displays any user who submitted a deletion request from the privacy modal.
- **Processing Erasure**:
  - Click **Process Erasure**.
  - A confirmation modal verifies permanent deletion.
  - Purges customer record, messages, and consents permanently.
  - Logs an entry in the Security Audit Trail for compliance records.

---

## 10. Module 9: Security & Audit Trail

The **Audit Trail** tab records an immutable chronological log:
- **Logged Actions**:
  - `ADMIN_LOGIN`: Admin authentication attempts and timestamps.
  - `UPDATE_PRODUCT_CATALOGUE`: Changes to product pricing or availability.
  - `UPDATE_ENQUIRY_STATUS`: Workflow transitions on customer orders.
  - `PROCESS_DATA_DELETION`: Compliance erasure events.
  - `EXPORT_CSV`: Data export events with record counts.
- **Searchable**: Search audit logs by action or record type.

---

## 11. Module 10: Retention & Compliance Policies

The **Retention & Policies** tab configures privacy parameters:
- **AI Chat Retention**: Configure how many days chat history is retained (default 90 days).
- **Telemetry Retention**: Configure how many days interaction events are kept (default 180 days).
- **Guest Storage Toggle**: Allow or disallow anonymous guest chat storage.
- **Auto-Purge Toggle**: Automatically purge records when a customer revokes consent.
